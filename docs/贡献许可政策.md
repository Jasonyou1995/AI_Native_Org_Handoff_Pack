# 贡献许可政策 / Contribution licensing policy

## 开放协作区：Apache-2.0 / Open collaboration: Apache-2.0

文档、规范、`tests/**`、`examples/**` 及许可清单中的其他 Apache 区域采用 Apache-2.0。提交者按该许可贡献，保留版权，不需要额外 CLA 或版权转让。Apache-2.0 的贡献与专利条款适用。维护者仍需核对来源和提交者授权能力。

Documentation, specifications, `tests/**`, `examples/**`, and other Apache areas in the scope map use Apache-2.0. Contributors submit under that license and retain ownership, without a separate CLA or copyright assignment. Apache-2.0 contribution and patent terms apply. Maintainers must still review provenance and the contributor's authority.

## 社区许可核心：明确非独占授权 / Community core: explicit nonexclusive grant

核心包括 `src/**`、`rust-core/**`、`demo/**` 及清单中的配置。外部核心实现或其他实质性受版权保护内容合并前，维护者与贡献者须明确记录适当的独立贡献协议，涵盖项目发行、修改及以社区和商业条款再许可该贡献所需的非独占版权授权与必要专利许可。范围必须限于贡献者有权授权的部分。

The core includes `src/**`, `rust-core/**`, `demo/**`, and listed configuration. Before merging external core implementation or other substantial copyrightable material, maintainers and contributors must explicitly document an appropriate separate contribution agreement covering nonexclusive copyright permissions and necessary patent rights for project distribution, modification, and relicensing under community and commercial terms. Its scope must be limited to rights the contributor can grant.

贡献者保留自己的版权，仍可使用和许可自己的贡献。协议应确认雇主权限、第三方材料及署名；不要求版权转让。本政策不是已签协议，也不因提交 PR、勾选行为准则或未反对就取得商业再许可权。协议未成立前不合并受此要求约束的核心贡献。组织、个人贡献者适用相同原则。

Contributors keep ownership and may continue using and licensing their contributions. The agreement should address employer authority, third-party material, and attribution; copyright assignment is not required. This policy is not an executed agreement. A PR, conduct acknowledgment, or silence does not grant commercial relicensing rights. Do not merge affected core contributions before agreement. The same principles apply to individuals and organizations.

## 不扩大权利 / No expansion of ownership

既有 Apache 授权不撤销。测试或示例调用核心不改核心许可。复制核心到开放区须先取得足够授权，不能用路径改变规避许可。独立插件仅调用公开 API 不自动归作者所有，也不自动认定为衍生作品；实际包含或转售核心功能时仍须核对核心条款。

Existing Apache grants are not revoked. Tests and examples do not relicense the core they call. Copying core into the open area requires sufficient rights; changing paths does not bypass licensing. Independent plugins do not automatically belong to the author or become derivative works just by calling public APIs; actual inclusion or resale of core functionality still requires review under the core terms.

AI 辅助不是权属证明。不得贡献第三方秘密、真实敏感数据或无权授权内容。商业及贡献协议宜由合格法律顾问结合实际情况审阅，本政策不保证法律结果或收入。

AI assistance is not proof of ownership. Do not contribute third-party secrets, real sensitive data, or material you cannot license. Commercial and contribution agreements should receive qualified legal review for their circumstances; this policy guarantees neither legal outcomes nor revenue.
