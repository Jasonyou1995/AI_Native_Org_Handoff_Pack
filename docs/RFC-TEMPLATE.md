# Boundary Change RFC · 边界变更提案

Status / 状态: Proposed / 提议中

## One problem and non-goals · 一个问题与非目标

State the necessary authorized result. Avoid expanding into a new platform.
说明必需且获准的成果，不扩张成另一套平台。

## Current and proposed boundary · 当前与拟议边界

Describe sources, recipients, purpose, permitted transformations, onward sharing, expiry and revocation limits.
描述来源、接收者、用途、允许转换、后续分享、有效期与撤销限制。

## Rights and operator assumptions · 权利与运营者假设

Identify who may authorize sharing and which parties must be trusted. Do not infer legal ownership from storage location.
说明谁有权批准分享、必须信任哪些参与者，不根据存储位置推断法律权属。

## Alternatives and costs · 替代方案与成本

Compare a simpler local-only path. Measure quality, latency, disclosure and operational burden separately.
对比更简单的仅本地方案，分别测量质量、延迟、披露范围及运维负担。

## Synthetic acceptance and failure tests · 合成验收与失败测试

Include denied, expired, revoked, malformed, repeated and interrupted requests. State checks not yet run.
覆盖被拒、过期、撤销、畸形、重复及中断请求，列明尚未运行的检查。

## Compatibility, licensing and rollback · 兼容、许可与回滚

Preserve existing grants and third-party notices. Explain migration and rollback without exposing private content.
保留既有授权与第三方声明，说明不暴露私人内容的迁移与回滚方式。

## Review and decision · 审查与决定

Record open objections, the accountable maintainer, scope of approval and follow-up evidence. A design decision does not grant missing contributor rights.
记录未解决异议、负责维护者、批准范围与后续证据；设计决定不补足缺失的贡献权利。
