# Commercial Licensing and Services · 商业授权与服务

## Model and purpose · 模式与目的

**Free normal internal use, an Apache collaboration ring, and separately licensed commercialization of the gateway core.** This keeps adoption accessible while giving maintenance a possible funding path. It does not charge people for retaining their own private knowledge or require them to disclose it. The [mission charter](13-使命与协作宪章.md) explains the privacy-first purpose.

**正常内部使用免费，Apache 协作层开放，网关核心商业化另行授权。** 这让采用保持低门槛，也给维护提供可能的资金来源。不因个人保留自己的私人知识而收费，也不要求交出这些知识。[使命宪章](13-使命与协作宪章.md)说明隐私优先的目的。

This page explains the existing license; it does not amend it. The English [Handoff Community 1.0](../LICENSES/Handoff-Community-1.0.txt) and [license scope](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md) determine the actual terms and covered materials. This custom source-available core license is **not OSI open source**. Existing Apache-2.0 rights and third-party terms remain intact.

本页说明现行许可，不修改条款。英文 [Handoff Community 1.0](../LICENSES/Handoff-Community-1.0.txt) 与[许可范围](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md)决定实际条款与适用材料。这份自定义源码可见核心许可**不是 OSI 开源许可**。既有 Apache-2.0 权利与第三方条款不变。

## When a separate license is needed · 何时需要另行授权

Individuals, universities, companies, nonprofits and other organizations may use the gateway internally for evaluation, research, teaching, development and production without contacting the author or paying a license fee. Internal profit, cost savings and normal university tuition do not change this.

个人、高校、企业、非营利组织及其他组织可免费将网关用于内部评估、研究、教学、开发和生产，无须先联系作者。内部业务获利、节约成本与高校正常学费不改变这一点。

| Scenario · 场景 | Separate commercial license · 另行商业授权 |
|---|---|
| Personal use or ordinary company internal operations · 个人自用、企业正常内部运营 | Not required · 不需要 |
| University research, teaching and student use with normal tuition · 高校研究、教学、学生使用及正常学费 | Not required · 不需要 |
| Compliant free redistribution/modification or free community services outside a commercial package · 合规免费分享修改版、非商业套餐中的免费社区服务 | Not required; retain license and notices · 不需要，须保留许可与通知 |
| Paid installation, configuration, training or support for a customer's own internal deployment · 为客户自有内部部署收费安装、配置、培训或支持 | Not required, provided this is not resale of access or paid hosted service · 不需要，但不得是转售访问或收费托管服务 |
| Ordinary work deliverables produced using the gateway internally · 内部使用网关后交付普通工作成果 | Not required if the customer is not receiving gateway software or functionality access · 不需要，客户不得因此获得网关软件或功能访问 |
| Selling gateway access to students or employees · 专门向学生或员工出售网关访问 | Required · 需要 |
| Selling/renting the gateway, paid SaaS or paid gateway hosting · 出售/出租网关、收费 SaaS 或收费网关托管 | Required · 需要 |
| Embedding or bundling the gateway in a commercial product for customer use · 将网关嵌入或捆绑对外商业产品 | Required even without a separate gateway line item · 需要，即使没有单独列出网关费用 |

Charging for a package while calling its gateway component “free” does not bypass the commercialization restriction. Ambiguous arrangements should be clarified against the actual license before launch, not decided by this summary alone.

收费套餐中把网关标成“免费”不能绕过商业化限制。边界不明的安排应在上线前依据实际许可厘清，不能仅凭本摘要作决定。

## Creator and service ecosystem · 创作者与服务生态

Public documents, specifications, standalone tests and examples form the Apache-2.0 collaboration ring. Gateway application code, Rust core including embedded tests, UI and the listed build/configuration files remain Community-licensed. See the exact [scope inventory](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md); open test fixtures do not relicense the implementation they exercise.

公开文档、规范、独立测试与示例构成 Apache-2.0 协作层。网关应用代码、Rust 核心及其内嵌测试、UI 和列明的构建配置继续适用 Community 许可。以[范围清单](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md)为准；开放测试夹具不改变其所调用实现的许可。

Creators may keep independently owned methods, prompts, models and knowledge bases private, and expose only an authorized capability through a reusable public interface. An independent API plugin is not automatically owned by this project or required to publish its private internals. Its own license, dependencies and rights still matter. Bundling the gateway or selling access to its functionality remains subject to the gateway's commercial terms.

创作者可将其独立拥有的方法、提示、模型与知识库保持私有，仅通过可复用公开接口提供获准能力。独立 API 插件不自动归本项目所有，也不因此必须公开私有内部实现。它自身的许可、依赖与权利仍需遵守。捆绑网关或销售其功能访问仍适用网关商业条款。

Optional paid integration, deployment, training, maintenance and support are legitimate service paths within the license. They are not conditions for free internal use. Compliant third-party providers may offer these services; the author does not claim an exclusive support monopoly. Creators and customers can negotiate service scope and compensation without assuming a transfer of all underlying know-how. Neither licensing nor the architecture guarantees demand, customers, revenue or employment protection.

在许可范围内，可选的付费集成、部署、培训、维护与支持是可行服务路径，不是免费内部使用的条件。合规第三方服务商也可提供这些服务；作者不主张独占支持市场。创作者与客户可协商服务范围和报酬，而不默认转移全部底层经验与方法。许可和架构都不保证需求、客户、收入或就业保护。

## Contributor rights · 贡献者权利

Apache-ring contributors retain copyright and contribute under Apache-2.0; no core-specific contribution agreement is required for that ring. For an accepted Community-core contribution, the recommended approach is a separately agreed **nonexclusive commercial relicensing grant**, sufficient for Community and commercial distribution, with applicable patent permissions and contributor ownership, attribution and independent reuse retained. No forced copyright assignment and no claim over unrelated private work.

Apache 协作层贡献者保留版权，按 Apache-2.0 贡献，无须该层之外的核心专用贡献协议。对拟接收的 Community 核心贡献，建议另行约定**非独占商业再许可授权**，覆盖 Community 与商业发行所需权利及适用专利许可，同时保留贡献者所有权、署名及独立复用权。不强迫转让版权，不索取无关私人作品的权利。

This recommendation is not an executed contributor agreement. Confirm authority, employer permissions and third-party provenance before merging core work, following the [contribution licensing policy](贡献许可政策.md). A PR, commit signature or this page does not itself give the maintainer commercial relicensing rights. The author licenses only rights they own or are authorized to grant; third-party work is not resold as if owned by the author.

此建议不是已签署的贡献协议。合并核心贡献前，按[贡献许可政策](贡献许可政策.md)核实授权能力、雇主权限与第三方来源。PR、提交签名或本页不自动赋予维护者商业再许可权。作者只许可自己拥有或获准授予的权利，不将第三方作品当作自己所有而再授权。

## Privacy and legal review · 隐私与法律审阅

The model does not depend on hidden telemetry, online activation, remote shutdown or uploading user content to the author. Source availability and self-hosting alone do not establish privacy or regulatory compliance. This is not legal advice or a guarantee of enforceability in every jurisdiction. Qualified counsel should review formal commercial and contribution agreements against the actual rights and use case.

此模式不依赖隐藏遥测、联网激活、远程停机或向作者上传用户内容。源码可见与自托管本身不证明隐私安全或法规合规。本页不是法律意见，也不保证所有司法辖区的可执行性。正式商业与贡献协议应由合格法律顾问结合实际权利与用途审阅。
