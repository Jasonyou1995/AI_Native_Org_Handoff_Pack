# 13 · Mission and Collaboration Charter · 使命与协作宪章

## One problem · 一个问题

**Keep private know-how local while sharing only the authorized information needed for productive AI collaboration.**

**让私有经验与方法留在本地，只共享高效 AI 协作所必需且已获授权的信息。**

This is our mission, not a claim that every boundary is implemented today. We want individuals, communities and organizations of every size to solve one problem together: productive AI collaboration without unnecessary extraction of personal know-how, methods or context.

这是我们的使命，不代表所有边界今天都已实现。我们希望个人、社区及不同规模的组织共同解决一个问题：在不额外抽取个人经验、方法与上下文的前提下，实现高效 AI 协作。

AI collaboration can create pressure to expose more of a person's working knowledge than a task needs. That risk motivates the project. We do not treat predictions about machine learning speed, jobs or economic systems as established facts. We aim to give people useful boundaries and choices, not promise a particular social outcome.

AI 协作可能带来压力，让个人交出超过任务所需的工作知识。这个风险是项目的动机。我们不把关于机器学习速度、就业或经济制度的预测当作既定事实。我们希望提供可用的边界与选择，不承诺特定社会结果。

## Principles · 原则

| Principle · 原则 | Practical meaning · 实际含义 |
|---|---|
| Privacy before efficiency · 隐私先于效率 | Optimize within an authorized boundary; never remove it to improve a metric. 在获准边界内优化，不为提高指标而取消边界。 |
| Deliverables are not source know-how · 成果不等于源知识 | An approved answer or result does not automatically authorize copying private notes, methods, memory, training data or hidden reasoning. 获准的答案或成果不自动授权复制私人笔记、方法、记忆、训练资料或隐藏推理。 |
| Respect rightful data boundaries · 尊重正当资料边界 | Personal storage does not establish ownership. Do not conceal employer-owned records or evade legitimate delivery or retention duties. Resolve disputed ownership before export. 存储在个人设备不决定权属，不得隐藏企业资料或规避正当交付与留存义务；权属有争议时先厘清，再外发。 |
| Narrow permissions at every hop · 每跳只缩不扩 | Purpose, recipients, actions and expiry stay within every source's grant; derived and mixed outputs inherit restrictions. Models and administrators cannot silently widen them. 目的、接收者、操作与到期时间不得超出来源授权；衍生与混合成果继承限制，模型和管理员不能悄悄扩权。 |
| Audit without surveillance · 审计不变成监视 | Record minimal permitted events and aggregate measures, not private work journals or a ranking of personal thinking. Agree retention and access in advance. 记录最少获准事件与汇总指标，不收集私人工作日记或对个人思考排名；事先约定留存与访问。 |
| Private capabilities, public interfaces · 能力可私有，接口可公开 | A plugin can return an authorized result without publishing its private implementation, prompts or knowledge base. Only contribute what you can authorize. 插件可返回获准成果，而不公开私有实现、提示或知识库；只贡献自己有权授权的内容。 |

These rules describe a conforming deployment and assume a trusted, correctly configured gateway; a hostile or compromised host administrator can bypass software controls, and this release does not protect against that administrator. Privacy is not ownership adjudication, secrecy from an employer-managed device administrator, or immunity from lawful obligations. Application approval is not a universal legal basis for workplace processing. Technical restrictions cannot retrieve already copied information or guarantee prevention of layoffs, leakage or misuse.

这些规则针对遵循协议的部署，前提是网关可信且配置正确；恶意或失陷的宿主管理员可绕过软件控制，本版本不提供对抗该管理员的保护。隐私不等于替人裁定产权、对企业设备管理员隐身，或免除合法义务。应用内批准不等于所有工作场景的合法处理依据。技术限制不能收回已复制信息，也不能保证避免裁员、泄漏或滥用。

## A chain of bounded work · 有边界的协作链

```text
Employee device -> approved necessary deliverable
Company gateway -> independently approved smaller cloud subtask
Cloud response -> company validation -> employee review / optional local restoration
```

**Employee device:** keep private context and mappings there; release only authorized necessary information. **Company:** combine the authorized deliverable with business records it may use, without gaining automatic access to personal methods. **Cloud:** receive only a separately approved, further-reduced task when local processing is insufficient and onward permission exists.

**员工设备：**保留私人上下文与映射，只交付必要且获准的信息。**企业：**将获准成果与其有权使用的业务资料结合，不自动取得个人方法。**云端：**仅在本地能力不足且具备继续转发权限时，接收独立批准、再次缩减的子任务。

Company-local is not employee-local. Device-side reduction must happen before company ingress. Approval to share with the company is separate from permission to forward to cloud; the company must also approve the exact cloud request. Each mapping stays at its originating layer. Returned or restored content is not automatically cleared for another export. If permission is missing, stay local or request a new explicit grant.

企业本地不等于员工本地，设备端缩减必须发生在企业接收之前。交给企业的批准与向云端转发的授权分开；企业还须批准确切云请求。各层映射留在原层。返回或回填后的内容不自动获得再次外发权限。缺少权限时留在本地，或重新请求明确授权。

This topology remains a proposal. The current gateway has no complete cross-hop identity, inherited authorization, distributed revocation or tenant isolation. See the [three-tier design](11-员工与企业三层交接.md) and [English specification](../spec/THREE-TIER-SHARING-SPEC.md).

这个拓扑仍是提案。当前网关尚无完整的跨跳身份、授权继承、分布式撤销或租户隔离。见[三层设计](11-员工与企业三层交接.md)与[英文规范](../spec/THREE-TIER-SHARING-SPEC.md)。

## How we collaborate · 怎样合作

**One accountable maintenance process, many small independent contributions.** A focused maintainer-led review model fits a security-sensitive gateway better than unrestricted merging. Individuals and large organizations use the same published review and acceptance criteria; sponsorship does not buy an exception to privacy or security gates.

**一套负责到底的维护流程，许多小而独立的贡献。** 对安全敏感的网关，由维护者负责审查比无限制合并更合适。个人与大型组织遵守同样公开的审查与验收标准；赞助不换取隐私或安全门槛的豁免。

1. **Propose a bounded problem.** Describe the authorized use case, trust boundary and synthetic pass/fail example. Keep private product logic and real customer data out.
2. **Use an RFC for boundary changes.** Changes to purpose, egress, identity, retention, ownership handling or license scope need a public written proposal, alternatives, review and an explicit maintainer decision before implementation or relicensing. Public discussion does not itself grant rights.
3. **Build a small unit.** Prefer an independent API adapter, documentation improvement or reproducible synthetic test over a large coupled feature. A plugin should declare inputs, outputs, permissions and local/network behavior, not require its creator's private knowledge source.
4. **Review and verify.** Maintainers own security review, provenance and license checks, regression gates and accurate release notes. Unrun Docker or browser checks remain pending. Report sensitive vulnerabilities privately through [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md), not in a public RFC.
5. **Merge with clear rights.** The Apache collaboration ring welcomes contributions under Apache-2.0 without a special core contribution agreement. Community-core contributions need separate explicit agreement before merging; otherwise feedback and non-mergeable suggestions remain welcome.

1. **提出有边界的问题。** 写明获准场景、信任边界与合成的通过/失败示例，不带入私人产品逻辑或真实客户数据。
2. **边界变更先走 RFC。** 目的、外发、身份、留存、权属处理或许可范围变更，先公开书面提案、替代方案与审查，再由维护者明确决定是否实施或变更许可。讨论本身不产生授权。
3. **做小而独立的单元。** 优先独立 API 适配器、文档改进与可复现合成测试，避免大型耦合功能。插件说明输入、输出、权限与本地/网络行为，不要求创作者交出私人知识来源。
4. **审查并验证。** 维护者负责安全审查、来源与许可核验、回归门槛及准确发布说明。未运行的 Docker 或浏览器检查仍标待验。敏感漏洞按[贡献指南](../CONTRIBUTING.md)私下报告，不放进公开 RFC。
5. **权利清楚再合并。** Apache 协作层按 Apache-2.0 接收贡献，无须核心专用贡献协议。Community 核心贡献须在合并前另行明确协议；否则仍欢迎反馈与不可直接合并的建议。

The Apache ring covers `docs/**`, `spec/**`, `tests/**` and `examples/**` under the [scope inventory](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md). The Community core includes `src/**`, `rust-core/**` including its embedded tests, `demo/**` and the listed build/configuration files. Calling core code from an Apache test does not relicense the core. Public standards and test fixtures support interoperability; their license does not certify a deployment.

Apache 协作层按[范围清单](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md)覆盖 `docs/**`、`spec/**`、`tests/**` 与 `examples/**`。Community 核心包含 `src/**`、`rust-core/**` 及其内嵌测试、`demo/**` 和列明的构建配置。Apache 测试调用核心代码不改变核心许可。公开标准与测试夹具有助互操作，但其许可不认证部署安全性。

**Recommended core contribution agreement:** a clearly disclosed, nonexclusive grant sufficient for Community distribution and commercial relicensing of the accepted contribution, including applicable patent rights, with contributor ownership, attribution and independent reuse retained. Confirm employer authority and third-party provenance. Do not require copyright assignment or rights to unrelated private work. The final terms need separate informed agreement and qualified legal review. This charter, a PR or a signature in a commit is not that agreement.

**建议的核心贡献协议：**明确告知并取得足以支持已接收贡献的 Community 发行与商业再许可的非独占授权，包括适用专利权，同时保留贡献者所有权、署名及独立复用权。核实雇主授权与第三方来源。不要求版权转让，不索取无关私人作品的权利。最终条款须另行知情同意并经合格法律审阅；本宪章、PR 或提交签名都不替代该协议。

## An ecosystem, not knowledge extraction · 生态不以抽取知识为前提

Keep reusable plumbing public and let creators control capabilities they have the right to control. They may share an adapter, offer a private plugin or sell a service without being required to publish their methods. Organizations can buy a useful authorized result or integration rather than demand a whole personal knowledge base. These are possible arrangements to negotiate, not promised compensation or automatic IP ownership.

公开可复用的基础设施，让创作者控制其有权控制的能力。他们可分享适配器、提供私有插件或出售服务，而不被强制公开方法。组织可购买有用且获准的成果或集成，而不是索取完整个人知识库。这些是可协商的安排，不是报酬承诺或自动产权认定。

Normal internal use remains free. Separate licensing for gateway resale, paid hosting and commercial bundling can fund the core; optional integration, training and support can fund delivery. Compliant third-party services remain welcome. Independently built API integrations do not automatically become the maintainer's property; a gateway bundle or paid access still follows the gateway license. No exclusive service monopoly, guaranteed buyers or passive-income promise. Details: [commercial model](商业授权.md).

正常内部使用保持免费。网关转售、收费托管及商业捆绑的单独授权可支持核心维护；可选的集成、培训与支持可支持交付。欢迎合规第三方服务。独立 API 集成不自动成为维护者的财产；捆绑网关或收费提供其访问仍须遵循网关许可。不建立独家服务垄断，不保证买家或被动收入。详见[商业模式](商业授权.md)。

## Evidence before claims · 先有证据，再作主张

Evaluate task success, disclosed information, boundary violations, latency, cost and approval burden together, using synthetic data and agreed workloads. Privacy constraints are pass/fail gates; a faster unsafe result is not success. Report limits and failures alongside improvements, without turning individual work traces into surveillance.

用合成数据与约定任务，同时评估任务成功率、披露量、越界情况、延迟、成本和审批负担。隐私约束是通过/失败门槛，更快但不安全不算成功。改进须与限制和失败一起报告，不把个人工作痕迹变成监视。

The recorded baseline has 47 TypeScript and 10 Rust tests passing. Docker and browser end-to-end gates remain pending; three-tier behavior is design only. See the [verification record](12-验证记录.md). This charter does not change executable behavior, grant unowned rights or promise legal compliance, job security or revenue.

已记录基线为 47 项 TypeScript 与 10 项 Rust 测试通过。Docker 与浏览器端到端门槛仍待验，三层行为仅为设计。见[验证记录](12-验证记录.md)。本宪章不改变运行行为、不授予无权授予的权利，也不保证合规、就业安全或收入。
